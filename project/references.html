Project Asset References

This page cites the external sources utilized for images, graphic assets, and data content across this application.

Trail Data & Content

  • Trail details, distances, and difficulty ratings for Donut Falls, Mount Timpanogos, Stewart Falls, Cecret Lake, and Bell Canyon were sourced from local trail guides on AllTrails.

Images & Visuals

  • The local image asset for Donut Falls (`donut-falls.jpg`) was sourced from user-submitted photography commons.
  • All other dynamic placeholder trail card images are loaded programmatically via the Lorem Picsum photos API platform.
  • Typography styles and iconography structures are delivered via Google Fonts (Montserrat & Open Sans).
